Privacy Policy
Last updated: 1 September 2026

What this covers
The OAuth client claude-nubit-clasp and the personal Google Apps Script projects it deploys.

What is accessed
The OAuth client is used by clasp, Google's own command line tool, to upload source code to Apps Script projects owned by the author of this repository. It authenticates as that single account and touches only that account's own Apps Script projects and their Drive files.

What is collected
Nothing. No analytics, no tracking, no third-party services, and no database. The deployed web apps hold their data either in the visitor's own browser or in a Google Sheet owned by the author.

What is shared
Nothing is sold, shared or transferred to anyone.

Retention
The OAuth credentials live on the author's machine and in a GitHub Actions secret used to deploy the projects. They can be revoked at any time from https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
